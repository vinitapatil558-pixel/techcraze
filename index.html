Reuse. Create. Inspire.

Waste can become
something wonderful.

Explore beginner-friendly projects that encourage people to reuse suitable household materials instead of throwing them away.

🪴

Plastic bottle planter

Turn a suitable, clean plastic bottle into a small planter. An adult should help with cutting.

  1. Clean and dry the bottle.
  2. Ask an adult to prepare the planter opening.
  3. Add suitable growing medium and a plant.
▶ Find a tutorial
📦

Cardboard desk organizer

Reuse a clean cardboard box to organize stationery and small supplies.

  1. Choose a clean, sturdy box.
  2. Arrange smaller cardboard dividers inside.
  3. Decorate it using materials you already have.
▶ Find a tutorial
🖊️

Glass jar pen holder

Give a clean, undamaged jar a new use as a holder for pens and pencils.

  1. Choose a clean jar with no cracks.
  2. Remove labels if they come off safely.
  3. Decorate the outside and add stationery.
▶ Find a tutorial

Make reuse part of everyday life.

Start with materials you already have. Choose safe projects and involve an adult whenever a project requires cutting or handling potentially sharp materials.

About SortSmart

Small actions.
Meaningful change.

SortSmart is a recycling concept designed to make waste sorting easier to understand and everyday reuse more inspiring.

🌍

The problem

People may be unsure how to separate different kinds of waste. Confusion can make responsible disposal harder.

💡

Our solution

SortSmart combines simple sorting guidance, a scanner prototype experience, and creative DIY ideas in one place.

⚙️

Our future vision

We envision exploring a physical sorting machine that could use cameras and sensors to assist with waste identification and sorting.

What makes SortSmart useful?

We designed the prototype around three priorities.

✓ Easy to understand

Simple navigation and clear suggestions help users find information.

✓ Encourages reuse

DIY inspiration helps users consider alternatives to throwing things away.

✓ Built around user needs

Sorting guidance and examples help address common household questions.

✓ A prototype that can grow

Future versions could be tested with users and improved based on feedback.
